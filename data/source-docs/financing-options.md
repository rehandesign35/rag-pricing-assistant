# Meridian Solar — Financing Options

*This document summarizes financing paths offered directly through Meridian Solar and its lending partners. Terms are illustrative and subject to credit approval.*

## 1. Cash Purchase

- Full ownership from day one; no monthly payments to Meridian or a lender.
- Eligible for the full value of all applicable tax credits and rebates (see `utility-incentives.md`).
- 5% discount applied at signing if paid in full before installation begins.
- Highest long-term savings of all options since there is no interest or lease markup.

## 2. Solar Loan

Offered through Meridian's lending partner, Brightpath Financial.

| Term | APR Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 10-year | 5.99% - 7.49% | Higher monthly payment, lowest total interest paid |
| 15-year | 6.49% - 7.99% | Most popular term; monthly payment often close to prior utility bill |
| 20-year | 6.99% - 8.49% | Lowest monthly payment, highest total interest paid |

- APR depends on credit score (loans available for credit scores 620+; best rates require 720+).
- $0 down payment options available; a down payment of 10-20% reduces the APR by approximately 0.5 percentage points.
- Customer owns the system and receives all tax credits and rebates directly (credits can be applied as a lump-sum payment against loan principal in year one).
- Example: a $24,500 Standard package financed over 15 years at 7.49% APR (no down payment) runs approximately $227/month before incentives are applied.
- No prepayment penalty.

## 3. Solar Lease

- Meridian (or a leasing partner) owns the system; customer pays a fixed monthly lease payment for the use of the system.
- Typical lease terms: 20 or 25 years, with an option to renew, purchase the system at fair market value, or have it removed at term end.
- Lease payments typically escalate 1.5-2.9% annually, specified in the individual lease agreement.
- The leasing company retains ownership and therefore claims the tax credit, not the customer — lease payments are structured to reflect this.
- Lower or no upfront cost; generally the lowest-savings option over the system's lifetime compared to cash or loan.
- Maintenance and equipment warranty coverage included for the lease duration.

## 4. Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)

- Customer pays only for the electricity the system actually produces, at a fixed rate per kWh (typically set below current utility rates at signing).
- No upfront cost; Meridian's PPA partner owns, monitors, and maintains the system.
- PPA rate is fixed for the first term (commonly 10 years) with a pre-agreed escalator (typically 2-3% per year) for the remainder of a 20-year agreement.
- Because production is metered and billed separately from the utility, customers on a PPA continue to receive a separate bill from their utility for any grid electricity used beyond what the system produces.
- Not available in all utility territories — eligibility depends on the customer's utility net metering policy (see `utility-incentives.md`).

## Choosing Between Options — Quick Comparison

| Priority | Best Fit |
|---|---|
| Maximum long-term savings | Cash purchase |
| Ownership + manageable monthly payment | Solar loan |
| Lowest possible upfront cost, don't want ownership responsibilities | Lease or PPA |
| Want to directly claim tax credits | Cash purchase or solar loan (not lease/PPA) |
