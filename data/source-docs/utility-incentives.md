## Net Metering Policy (Fictional utility: Cascade Power & Light)

- Under Cascade Power & Light's net metering program, excess solar production is credited to the customer's account at the **full retail electricity rate**, banked as a kWh credit.
- Banked credits roll over month to month and reset annually each April billing cycle; unused credits at reset are paid out at the **avoided-cost wholesale rate** (approximately 30-40% of retail rate), not full retail.
- Systems must be sized at or under 120% of the home's trailing 12-month average consumption to qualify for full net metering; oversized systems are billed at wholesale rate for excess production above that threshold.
- Net metering enrollment requires interconnection approval, which Meridian files on the customer's behalf as part of the base installation package.

## Time-of-Use Rate Schedule (Cascade Power & Light, Residential)

| Period | Hours | Rate ($/kWh) |
|---|---|---|
| Off-Peak | 10 PM - 6 AM daily | $0.11 |
| Mid-Peak | 6 AM - 4 PM, 9 PM - 10 PM | $0.19 |
| On-Peak | 4 PM - 9 PM daily | $0.34 |

- Customers on the standard (non-TOU) rate pay a flat $0.21/kWh regardless of time of use.
- Solar production peaks midday (mid-peak pricing), so battery storage is typically recommended for customers on TOU rates to shift stored solar energy into the on-peak window rather than exporting it at mid-peak credit rates.

## Federal Incentive (Illustrative — Reference "Residential Clean Energy Credit")

- Illustrative federal credit modeled at **30% of total system cost** (equipment + installation), claimed as a tax credit in the year the system is placed in service.
- Applies to cash purchases and solar loans (system owned by the customer); does not apply to leases or PPAs, where the third-party owner claims the credit instead.
- No maximum dollar cap modeled in this illustrative scenario.
- Customers should confirm actual, current-year eligibility and percentage with a licensed tax professional — this figure is a placeholder for evaluation purposes only.

## State Rebate Program (Fictional: Evergreen State Solar Rebate)

| Household Income Bracket | Rebate |
|---|---|
| Under $75,000/year | $2,500 flat rebate |
| $75,000 - $150,000/year | $1,000 flat rebate |
| Over $150,000/year | Not eligible |

- Rebate is applied as a check mailed within 60-90 days of system inspection sign-off, not as an upfront discount.
- Rebate is stackable with the federal incentive and with financing (loan/cash), but not available to lease or PPA customers since they do not own the system.
- Program has an annual funding cap; applications are processed first-come, first-served each fiscal year (July-June) and may pause if funds are exhausted before year-end.
