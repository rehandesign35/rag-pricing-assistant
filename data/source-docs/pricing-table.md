## Price Match & Discounts

- Meridian offers a 5% discount for full cash payment at signing (not combinable with financing offers).
- Referral credit: $500 off for both referring customer and new customer, applied at installation.
- Standard package prices are locked for 30 days from quote date; custom/à la carte quotes are valid for 14 days due to equipment cost fluctuation.
